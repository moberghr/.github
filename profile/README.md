Moberg is a software engineering studio in **Reykjavík and Zagreb**. We build the systems businesses run on — and over the past year we've rebuilt *how* that software gets made, turning our own engineering discipline into open tooling that keeps AI-assisted development honest.

We don't just ship code. We build solutions that make a difference, with people at the center of everything we do.

<br>

| Software engineering | Data &amp; AI |
