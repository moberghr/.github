Moberg is a software engineering studio based in **Reykjavík and Zagreb**. We build the systems businesses run on, and over the past year we have extended that same engineering discipline into AI-assisted development, releasing the tooling we built for our own teams as open source.

We build solutions that make a difference, with people at the center of everything we do.

<br>

### `01`&nbsp;&nbsp;What we do

| Software engineering | Data &amp; AI |
|---|---|
| Discovery and product strategy, UX/UI, web and mobile, cloud and platform engineering. Long-lived systems built to be maintained, not throwaway MVPs. | Data engineering and BI, data science, applied AI and agentic systems, governance and strategy. Turning data into decisions people can trust. |

### `04`&nbsp;&nbsp;How we work

- **Serious engineering.** We treat software as infrastructure. Correctness, security, and review discipline are the default, now enforced in code rather than left to principle.
- **Long-term partnerships.** We embed as an extension of your team and stay for the long run, through ongoing maintenance as much as the initial launch.
- **A respectful, people-first approach.** People are at the center of everything we build: users, clients, and engineers alike.
